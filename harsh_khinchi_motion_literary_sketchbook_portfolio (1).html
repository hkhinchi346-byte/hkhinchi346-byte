About Me • Artist's Note

The Seam Between Craft & Motion.

Indian Institute of Crafts & Design (IICD), Jaipur • B.Des Batch of 2030

My path into visual storytelling didn't begin inside a conventional film school—it started with ledgers, late-night notebooks, and a fascination with how things are built by hand. After completing my CBSE Class 12 Commerce studies at Maheshwari Public School, Jaipur (2025), I entered the B.Des in Crafts & Design at IICD Jaipur, where raw material, cotton thread, and paper are treated as living languages.

Before any frame moves on screen, it begins as ink on parchment or black cardstock under a blade. Whether I am stitching continuous-line textile patches in the embroidery studio, photographing articulated silhouettes for stop-motion films like BURNOUT, or layering glitch textures over evergreen melodies, I believe digital art feels most human when it preserves the grain of the hand that made it.

Alongside visual direction and stage anchoring at IICD's cultural Varta sessions, I write Hindi, English, and bilingual Hinglish verse—featured in anthologies by Storymonk International, Groww Foundation's Imprint, and India Writing Project. Inside the 3D Sketchbook below, you can turn through my poetry leaf by leaf.
